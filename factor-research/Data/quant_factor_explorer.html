Cumulative-window definitions — return-type series (FF factors, asset classes): compounded return over the trailing N months, ((1+r₁/100)×…×(1+rₙ/100) − 1)×100. Macro PCA factors: simple sum of the trailing N monthly z-scores (they're z-scored composites, not returns, so summing captures the cumulative "impulse" rather than a compounded growth rate). N is 3 or 6 depending on the mode selected.

Series come straight from Data/macro_pca_factors.csv, Data/FF_plus_macro_workable.csv (Mkt-RF, SMB, HML, RMW, CMA), and Data/asset_class_monthly_returns.csv (raw monthly total returns, not excess-of-RF). Correlation is Pearson's r on the overlapping non-null pairs after the lag shift and any cumulative transform.
